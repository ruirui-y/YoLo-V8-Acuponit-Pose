## 8. Depth 当前参数

```text
depth_low  = 1100 mm
depth_high = 1850 mm
```

无效值：

```text
(~isfinite) | (depth <= 0) | (depth == 65535)
```

第四通道：

```text
ch4 = 1 + (clip(depth, 1100, 1850) - 1100) * 254 / 750
```
